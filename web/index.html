Not an official warning

APAW is an educational portfolio project, not a flood advisory. PAGASA and your local disaster risk reduction office decide what counts as a warning and when water is released — follow them, not this page. The forecasts here come from a small statistical model that is often wrong, and every one of its errors is published above.
