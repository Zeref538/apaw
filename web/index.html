Pulso forecasts how full the major dams will get, from PAGASA's daily bulletin and Open-Meteo rainfall. It learns from one observation at a time — and publishes the horizons where a naive baseline still beats it.

Not an official warning

Pulso is an educational portfolio project, not a flood warning and not an operational advisory. PAGASA and your local disaster risk reduction office are the authorities on dam releases and flooding — follow them, not this page. These forecasts come from a small statistical model that is often wrong, and its errors are published above so you can judge for yourself.
